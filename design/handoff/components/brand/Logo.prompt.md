Smile Lab logo — use the lockup on every ad; symbol alone only for square logo assets and avatars.

```jsx
<Logo variant="lockup" size={56} />
<Logo variant="lockup" size={56} tone="dark" />
<Logo variant="symbol" size={520} />
```

- Never place it loose on a detailed photo: sit it on a #ffffff / #f6fafa bar or card, or on #22343a.
- Clear space: half the symbol width on every side. Min symbol height on a 1080 canvas: 48px.
- Gap symbol→wordmark is 13/42 of the symbol height. Never stretch, recolor or add effects.
